Before the AI changes the model

  • It sends the words you typed, and the words the model was first built from, to the AI provider, Anthropic, through Claude Code. Nothing else in BrickSnap sends anything off your computer.
  • It uses your own Claude Code login, so it counts towards your Claude subscription.
  • It is one AI run, starting from the model as it is now. It can take as long as building it did; you can leave this page while it works.
  • The model as it is now stays in the list of runs below, so you can always bring it back.
  • This is experimental. Studs, pins, axles, bars, wheels and clips on bars are checked for fit. Parts joined by hinges and ball joints are counted as joined without proof, and nothing judges yet how strong a joint is.

What moves

    See it with everything opened

    The checker makes sure every part is held, nothing overlaps and the model stands. It checks studs, pins, axles, bars, wheels and clips on bars for fit, counts parts joined by hinges and ball joints as joined without proof, and does not judge how strong a joint is. When the model says what moves, it also checks that each moving part is free to move.
