Before the AI starts building

  • It uses your own Claude Code login, so it counts towards your Claude subscription.
  • It is one AI run. It usually takes ten to twenty minutes; you can leave this page while it works.
  • This is experimental. A checker makes sure every part is held, nothing overlaps and the model stands. It checks studs, pins, axles, bars, wheels and clips on bars for fit. But parts joined by hinges and ball joints are counted as joined without proof, and nothing judges yet how strong a joint is.
