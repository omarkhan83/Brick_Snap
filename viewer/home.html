BrickSnap

What should the AI build?

Experimental Describe it, add pictures or a 3D model of it, or both. The AI builds it part by part from the whole LEGO® parts library (wheels, leaves, wings, windscreens), checking and looking at its work as it goes.

    Up to 4 pictures (photos or drawings: JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 8 MB each, 20 MB in all) and one 3D model (STL, OBJ, GLB, GLTF, 3MF or PLY, up to 60 MB). Drop them on the box, or use the button. Pictures are kept with the model, made smaller and without the details photos carry, such as where they were taken.

    More about the time and cost

    Options
    200 parts

    A big model. Big models take longer and use more of your subscription.

    Fable 5.2 builds the more refined models. Opus 5.5 uses about half as much of your subscription. If one has reached its usage limit, choose the other.

    The AI first writes an art direction (the scale, two or three colours, the shapes that make the subject), then builds a core with sides pressed on, as official sets are built, and judges its own pictures against a style guide before it finishes. Later changes to the model keep this choice.

    After the build, an outside eye (a separate AI shown only the pictures) lists up to five fixes, and the AI makes them as one change. It roughly doubles the time and the cost.

    This is experimental. A checker makes sure every part is held, nothing overlaps and the model stands. It checks studs, pins, axles, bars, wheels and most clips on bars for fit. But parts joined by hinges, ball joints and the other clips are counted as joined without proof, and nothing judges yet how strong a joint is.
