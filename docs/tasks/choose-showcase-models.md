# Choose three showcase models and order parts for one
- status: open
- owner: nobody
- stage: 3

Pick the three models the two-minute walkthrough will use (see `docs/ROADMAP.md`, stage 4). They should
show different strengths: something blocky with a face (the cat), something rounded (the duck), and
something a person brought themselves (a scan, a CAD part, or a logo). For one of them, export the wanted
list and order the parts, because parts take time to arrive.

Done when the three are named here with a picture each, and an order has been placed or the group has
decided not to build one physically.

## Notes
No code needed. Ordering needs a person with a BrickLink account and a decision about who pays. Models of
copyrighted things are not shipped as samples (see `docs/VISION.md`).
