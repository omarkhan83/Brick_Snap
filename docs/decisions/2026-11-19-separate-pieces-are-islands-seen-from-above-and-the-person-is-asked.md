# A file's separate pieces are the islands it makes seen from above, and the person is asked what to build
- status: accepted
- by: Alec-42, 2026-11-19

Some model files are a kit laid out for printing, not one object. Seen from above, the parts of a kit
lie side by side with clear ground between them, so each island is a piece (unless it is tiny or flat),
and two or more is put to the person as a question, never decided for them: one piece alone, or
everything together. Rejected after an independent review broke each: grouping the mesh's separate
bodies by their boxes (it split one double-sided surface into six and glued real parts together), and
asking that pieces stand on one ground level (the parts of the real file that prompted this lie at four
levels). Knowingly asked about, because the answer is one click: letters or beads laid flat, and a
sizeable detail floating beside (not above) a figure.
