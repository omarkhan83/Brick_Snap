# Statistics worked out from Rebrickable's catalogue may be used, with credit, including in the AI's guide
- status: accepted
- by: Alec-42, 2026-11-19

The study of how real sets are built counted kinds of part over the parts lists of 5,935 sets from
Rebrickable's published catalogue. Rebrickable's terms ask for credit and say that none of its content
may be used to train AI models. We train nothing: we computed totals and wrote guidance from them, and
the guide an AI builder reads carries a few of those totals (how many different parts a small set uses,
when a frame of beams starts to appear). Alec's reading is that this is outside what the term is there to
prevent, and this is a class project. What we keep to: Rebrickable is credited wherever the figures
appear; only totals are used, never the parts list of a set; and the catalogue files themselves stay out
of the repository.
